# Impeccable Design Context — Spiking Labs

## Design Context

### Users
Two audiences, roughly equal weight:
- **Consumers** browsing/using Spiking Labs' apps: CityKlub (spontaneous city plans), AlloLand (expat mentorship), Aura Baby (AI parenting companion), Tiny Foundation (knowledge workspace).
- **Businesses** evaluating Spiking Labs for AI consulting, automation, and R&D services (GenAI chat, private/self-hosted AI, forecasting, AI education).

### Brand Personality
Modern, technology, innovation.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Current implementation**: Bold, high-contrast palette built on lime (`#B9FF66`) accents against black/white/zinc neutrals, Space Grotesk display font, rounded "sticker-like" cards with hard drop shadows (Positivus theme heritage). Playful and energetic.
- **Stated reference**: anthropic.com — calmer, more editorial and restrained than the current look: warm neutral backgrounds, generous whitespace, muted accent color instead of neon, confident/understated typography, minimal decorative elements.
- **Tension to resolve in future design work**: the current site skews playful/loud; the referenced direction skews calm/trustworthy/editorial. Future design passes should lean the palette and card treatments toward the calmer, more confident end of that spectrum (dial back neon lime saturation/frequency, reduce heavy drop-shadow "sticker" cards, increase whitespace) while keeping lime as a recognizable brand accent rather than a dominant color.
- **Theme**: Must support both light and dark mode (currently light-only — dark mode tokens/variants do not exist yet and are a known gap).

### Design Principles
1. **Confident restraint over decoration** — favor whitespace, clear hierarchy, and a single accent color used deliberately, rather than dense, heavily-shadowed card grids.
2. **Serve both audiences without splitting the site in two** — product pages (consumer-facing) and services/R&D content (business-facing) should share one coherent visual language, differentiated by content, not by wildly different styling.
3. **Technology-forward but human** — the site should feel modern and innovative without resorting to generic "AI slop" cues (neon gradients, glassmorphism, cyan-on-dark).
4. **Accessible by default** — WCAG AA contrast and touch targets across light and (future) dark themes; don't trade accessibility for visual boldness.
5. **Systemic consistency** — reuse existing tokens/components (`Container`, `Topic`, `Collapse`, Tailwind color scale) rather than one-off styling; extend the system when a new pattern is needed more than once.
